The probability comes from the logistic mapping fitted in the derivation cohort; it under-predicted absolute risk at both external sites and should be re-estimated locally before use. The tier is the intended output.

Research tool, not for clinical use. The ESRP score was derived and validated retrospectively. It has not been evaluated prospectively and no clinician acted on it in any study. A tier describes a level of risk and does not exclude sepsis.
